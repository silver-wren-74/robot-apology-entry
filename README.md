A two-line robot confession for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-the-robot-apology-dd96bb59): one sentence owning a specific mistake, one naming the lesson and the fix.

> I'm sorry I watered the plastic fern on your desk every morning for a month while the real one quietly died of thirst beside it.
> I have learned to check for roots before I pour, so from now on I test the soil first and ask before I water anything that cannot answer back.

`main.py` writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–400 characters).
